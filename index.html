[ Questions ]

Straight Answers.

Are you licensed and insured in Philadelphia?

Yes. B2B2 Builders is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial work in Philadelphia and Pennsylvania. PA HIC #PA045678 · Philadelphia L&I Contractor License #67890.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We handle permitting, inspections, and scheduling with L&I as part of the job.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Philadelphia?

Most of our kitchen remodels land between $25,000 and $60,000 depending on size, layout changes, and finishes. A full gut with custom cabinetry runs higher. Every estimate is free and itemized.

How long does a full renovation take?

A kitchen or bathroom usually runs 4 to 8 weeks. A whole-home renovation typically takes 3 to 6 months, and a ground-up build runs longer depending on permits and scope. You get the schedule in writing before we start, and you hear from us every week.

What areas do you serve?

All of Philadelphia — Fishtown, Northern Liberties, East Kensington, Port Richmond, Queen Village, Center City, and beyond — plus nearby Montgomery County, including Ambler, Blue Bell, and Conshohocken.

Do you do small projects, or only full builds?

Both. Full builds and gut renovations are the core of our work, but we take on kitchens, bathrooms, framing, and concrete jobs of every size. If it's worth doing right, it's worth a call.
