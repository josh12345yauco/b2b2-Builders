About This Project

Finishing work at a brick home, shown the way it actually happens. The porch was closed in with new windows and an entry door, hung with moisture-resistant drywall, taped, trimmed, and painted — keeping the original brick wall and beadboard ceiling.

Inside, a bedroom was taken back to the wood lath and re-boarded, walls and ceiling. The in-progress photos show the taping stage — the part of the job that decides whether the finished walls read flat under paint.

Finishing is where a renovation gets judged. Straight corners, tight trim, and seams you can't find are the standard.
