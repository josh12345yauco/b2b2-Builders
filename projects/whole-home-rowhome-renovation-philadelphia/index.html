Neighborhood
Philadelphia
Project type
Property Rehab
Scope

About This Project

A Philadelphia rowhome renovated from the front door to the back fence. The first floor now runs open from the living room to the kitchen on wide-plank floors, with the original brick left exposed at the chimney and along the rebuilt staircase.

The kitchen sits under a tray ceiling: white shaker cabinetry, a black granite island, a pro-style gas range under a stainless hood, and floating wood shelves over a subway tile backsplash. Upstairs, the primary bath pairs a freestanding tub with a dark herringbone tile shower and a walnut double vanity, and the hall bath carries brass fixtures over a tiled tub surround.

Out back, glass doors open to a composite deck with black railing that steps down to a bluestone patio enclosed by a horizontal board fence. Inside and out, one crew carried the job — which is why the details line up from room to room.
