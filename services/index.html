[ FAQs ]

Looking for Some Answers?

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes — all of it. Building permits through Philadelphia L&I or your township, licensed-trade sign-offs for electrical and plumbing, and every inspection through final approval. Permits are part of the scope on every job, never your homework.

How long do projects take?

Bathrooms run 2–4 weeks, kitchens 3–10 weeks depending on scope, full gut renovations 4–6 months, and ground-up builds longer. Whatever the project, the schedule is written into your proposal before we start — and you get an update every week.

Can you work with a tighter budget?

Often, yes — honestly. Because our estimates are itemized, we can show you exactly where money goes and where scope can flex: keeping a layout instead of moving plumbing, semi-custom instead of custom cabinets, phasing work in the right order. What we won't do is hit a number by cutting what's behind the walls.

Is your work warrantied?

Yes. Our workmanship is covered in writing, and manufacturer warranties on materials and fixtures pass through to you with the closeout paperwork. If something we built fails, you call us and we fix it.
